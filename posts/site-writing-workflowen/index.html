Example: How I Maintain the Content on This Site

A clearly labeled sample article that demonstrates the writing layout and bilingual publishing workflow.

PublishedSeptember 12, 20261 min readExample content
On this page
  1. Start with a short draft
  2. Check before publishing
  3. Keep the content maintainable

This is a clearly labeled example article. It exists to demonstrate the reading experience and can be replaced or removed later.

A personal website can do more than present finished work. It can also serve as a living research record where publications, experience, and developing ideas remain connected.

Start with a short draft

I begin with a Markdown file containing the title, summary, and article body. Metadata and prose live together, so changing the date, language, or tags does not require an admin interface.

---
title: A new article
lang: en
tags: [Research notes]
---

The article begins here.

Check before publishing

Before publishing, I check a short list: whether the title is accurate, links work, images have useful descriptions, and the Chinese and English entry points lead to pages that exist.

Bilingual articles share a translationKey. When an article has no translation yet, the page links to the other language’s blog index instead of presenting a broken translation link.

Keep the content maintainable

Publication data, experience, and blog posts use separate structures. Updating the CV does not disturb an article, and editing the blog does not change the publication list. Once the real material is ready, placeholder data can be replaced without rebuilding the site.
